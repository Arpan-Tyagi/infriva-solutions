2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO & Indexability (Weight: 22%)

Score: 59/100

What Works

  • Next.js 16 App Router with Static Site Generation (generateStaticParams) across all 7 service pages, 10 blog posts, and 2 project case studies
  • HTTPS enforced with HSTS (max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains), X-Frame-Options: DENY, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, and Referrer-Policy headers
  • Proper HTTP 404 status returned via notFound() on invalid /services/[slug], /blog/[slug], and /projects/[slug] routes
  • Zero-CLS font pipeline using next/font/google (Geist Sans & Geist Mono) and accessible Skip-to-Content bypass link

Findings

C1: Canonical Host Split (non-www vs www) & Missing <link rel='canonical'> Critical

Live infrastructure 307-redirects https://infrivasolutions.com to https://www.infrivasolutions.com, but layout.tsx, sitemap.ts, and robots.ts default to https://infrivasolutions.com and omit alternates.canonical.

Recommendation: Standardize canonical origin to https://www.infrivasolutions.com across layout.tsx, sitemap.ts, and robots.ts, and configure self-referencing alternates.canonical on every static and dynamic route.

C1b: Broken /privacy URL in Sitemap & Uniform Build-Time lastModified Timestamps Critical

src/app/sitemap.ts lists /privacy (which returns HTTP 404) instead of /privacy-policy, and stamps every URL with new Date() on build, neutralizing crawl prioritization.

Recommendation: Update /privacy to /privacy-policy in src/app/sitemap.ts and source real ISO publication/modification dates from unified data modules in src/lib/.

M4: Missing Permissions-Policy & Content-Security-Policy Headers Medium

next.config.ts defines basic security headers but omits Permissions-Policy and Content-Security-Policy.

Recommendation: Add Permissions-Policy (camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()) and a production-safe Content-Security-Policy header in next.config.ts.

2.2 Content Quality & E-E-A-T (Weight: 23%)

Score: 52/100

What Works

  • Cohesive Brutalist-Luxury visual and editorial identity across the main landing pages
  • Quantitative outcome metrics highlighted in FlyingLyte (+40% leads, -55% drop-off) and StarX Hotel (+35% direct bookings, ~18% OTA commission saved)
  • Transparent INR pricing tiers (₹20,000/mo to ₹90,000+) and SLA commitments published on service detail pages

Findings

H2a: Thin Service Detail Pages (~180 Words vs 800+ B2B Benchmark) High

Each of the 7 /services/[slug] pages contains only a single overview sentence, 3 metric pills, and 4 bullet titles with no architectural breakdown, tech stack details, deliverables narrative, or service Q&A.

Recommendation: Expand all 7 service pages in a shared src/lib/services.ts registry to 800+ words with architectural breakdowns, phase-by-phase execution deliverables, technology stack, linked case study proof, and Q&A.

H2b: Thin /about Page (~140 Words) with Zero Founder/Team E-E-A-T Signals High

/about contains no founder or leadership bios, no engineering credentials, no company founding story, and no physical operational footprint.

Recommendation: Expand /about to 500+ words including leadership/architect profiles, engineering methodology, verifiable metrics, and governance principles.

H2c: Blog Posts Lack Author Bylines, B2B Depth & Internal Links to Services/Case Studies High

All 10 blog articles are published anonymously without author bylines/bios, contain zero internal links to /services/[slug] or /projects/[slug], and several use B2C examples (salons, perfumes) rather than B2B system architecture.

Recommendation: Add named author bylines and credential bios, enrich articles with B2B architectural insights, and weave contextual internal links to relevant service and case study pages.

M2: Email Discrepancy on Privacy Policy & Missing NAP Details on /contact Medium

src/app/privacy-policy/page.tsx lists infrivasolutions@gmail.com while Footer.tsx uses info@infrivasolutions.com. /contact lacks visible phone numbers, email address, and headquarters location.

Recommendation: Standardize email to info@infrivasolutions.com across all pages and display complete NAP (Name, Address/Location, Phone, Email, Working Hours) on /contact and Footer.tsx.

2.3 On-Page SEO & Metadata Architecture (Weight: 20%)

Score: 48/100

What Works

  • Dynamic generateMetadata implemented on /services/[slug], /projects/[slug], and /blog/[slug]
  • Clean, descriptive, keyword-rich URL slugs across all 27 routes

Findings

H1a: 6 Static Routes Share Identical Root Title & 59-Character Description High

/, /services, /projects, /about, /blog, and /contact all output 'Infriva | Premium Digital Architecture' and 'We engineer digital experiences with cinematic spatial rhythm.' because page-level metadata is missing and /projects/page.tsx is a Client Component.

Recommendation: Extract client parallax cards from /projects/page.tsx into ProjectsGrid.tsx and export unique title, description, openGraph, twitter, and alternates.canonical metadata across all 8 static routes.

H1b: Blog Meta Descriptions Truncated Mid-Word & Date/Category/Image Drift High

src/app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx slices stripped HTML at 160 chars instead of using curated descriptions, and disagrees with src/app/blog/page.tsx on dates, categories, and images for multiple posts.

Recommendation: Consolidate blog data into src/lib/blog.ts with curated 140–160 char descriptions, ISO dates, and unified images/categories.

H2d: Skipped Heading Hierarchy (H1 -> H3 with Zero H2s) on Blog & Service Pages High

/services/[slug] and all 10 /blog/[slug] articles jump directly from H1 to H3, and Homepage uses H3 ('Modern Tech Stack', 'The Engineering Process') for top-level sections without parent H2s.

Recommendation: Promote top-level section headings to H2 and subsections to H3 across Homepage, /services/[slug], and all 10 blog articles.

2.5 Performance & Core Web Vitals (Weight: 10%)

Score: 64/100

What Works

  • Zero layout shift (CLS ~0.012) thanks to next/font and aspect-ratio containers
  • Fast server TTFB via static route generation

Findings

C2: 2.5s Blocking Preloader & Multi-Second Hero Animation Delays Critical

Preloader.tsx blocks viewport for 2,500ms with body overflow:hidden, while Hero.tsx delays H1 and CTA by 2.2s–2.7s and concatenates words in SSR HTML without spaces. Ticker.tsx renders 0+ in SSR HTML.

Recommendation: Bypass blocking Preloader curtain for initial render, remove multi-second Hero delays, ensure H1 and above-the-fold content render at opacity:1 in SSR HTML, and initialize Ticker.tsx with target values.

H3a: 7.35 MB Hero Video Without Branded Poster Fallback & Chatbot Bundle Weight High

Hero.tsx loads /hero-bg-v2.mp4 (7.35 MB) with an inline blank SVG poster and eagerly bundles react-markdown + remark-gfm in Chatbot.tsx on every route even when the chat drawer is closed.

Recommendation: Use a lightweight WebP/JPG poster fallback in Hero.tsx, respect prefers-reduced-motion, lazy-load Chatbot markdown rendering, and offset the mobile Chatbot trigger button.

  • Dedicated blog article on SEO, AEO, and GEO strategy

https://www.infrivasolutions.com/llms.txt returns 404 and robots.ts lacks explicit allow rules for GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, and Google-Extended.
